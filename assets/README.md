# Garment photography

Placeholder photography used inside the app mockups (hero collage, wardrobe
grid, scan screen, swipe card). Wired up in `style.css` via the `--tile`,
`--tint` and `--tile-bg` custom properties — override `--tile` per slot to swap
one for a real app screenshot.

All photos come from [Burst](https://burst.shopify.com) (Shopify) and are used
under the [Burst license](https://burst.shopify.com/licensing): free for
commercial use, no attribution required. They were cropped to square (or to the
target aspect ratio) and re-encoded as WebP at quality 76.
