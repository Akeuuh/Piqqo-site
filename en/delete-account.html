Deleting your Piqqo account

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Piqqo is published by Lexa - Axel Leclercq (sole proprietor / entrepreneur individuel).

From the app

  1. Open Piqqo and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings > My data.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Without access to the app

Email piqqo@lexa.bzh from your account's email address, asking for deletion. We process the request within 30 days.

Data deleted

Account and credentials, clothes and photos, generated outfits, favorites, history, preferences, audience-measurement events.

Data retained

Billing records tied to purchases: kept for up to 10 years to meet legal accounting obligations, then deleted. Audience-measurement events, already anonymized, are purged within 13 months maximum.

See also our privacy policy.
